Who you'll work with

Codegrity was founded in 2023 by PJ Sojdei, a senior .NET and full-stack engineer with more than 15 years in production software. He led a team of more than ten developers at Nerium (now Neora), worked as a solution architect at Nokia, and has spent recent years as the engineer companies call when an integration fails in production.
