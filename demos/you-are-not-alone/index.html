Why this exists

You are loved. We are His hands.

Be the light for someone this week.

Hard times happen to everybody, and often we feel like we're the only one. Whatever the struggle is, big or small, it's real and it's hard.

You Are NOT Alone is a simple way to show the people around us that we love them, one basket at a time.

  • Stress at work
  • Raising kids
  • Marriage
  • Friendships
  • School
  • Sports

Who the baskets are for

You can nominate a friend or family member in one of three groups.

Moms

Carrying everyone else and running on empty.

Boys, 11 to 17

Dealing with school, friends, sports or something they haven't said out loud yet.

Girls, 11 to 17

Going through a hard stretch and needing to know someone noticed.

How it works

Nominating someone takes about two minutes.

  1. Tell us about them

    Their name, which group they're in, their shirt size and a few of their favorite things.

  2. We build their basket

    Each basket is put together around the hobbies and interests you share.

  3. We let you know

    If your friend is chosen, we reach out to you first so the basket can be a surprise.
