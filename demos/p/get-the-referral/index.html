Three fixes, shown above.

Each one is small on its own. Together they make the page clearer to a buyer and easier for Google and AI search tools to understand.

1

Fix 1: Put your biggest proof next to the headline

On your site today

"Trusted by 500+ home services companies" is a single line with nothing to back it up, and your reach across 35 states, Puerto Rico, Canada and Australia is only on the About page.

In this preview

The 500+ figure up top with your reach as its source line, beside a customer quote. Buyers believe a number when it comes with context.

2

Fix 2: Testimonials a contractor can trust

On your site today

All eight testimonials show a first name and last initial only, like "Kyle H." and "Nick R.", with no company or trade. A roofer can't tell whether any of them run a business like theirs.

In this preview

Each quote with the person's company and trade. The dashed label shows where it goes.

3

Fix 3: FAQ answers that match their questions

On your site today

Three answers appear under the wrong question. "How do we pay referral rewards?" is answered with app launch timing, and "How much time do we need for onboarding?" is answered "Yes, we recommend it."

In this preview

Each question with its correct answer, written from what your own site already says about GTR Wallet, onboarding and integrations.
