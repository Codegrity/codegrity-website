Three fixes, shown above.

Each one is small on its own. Together they make the page clearer to a buyer and easier for Google and AI search tools to understand.

1

Fix 1: One clear promise at the top

On your site today

The page title and the main headline both read "Stop Grinding. Start Scaling. More Profit. Less Chaos. Finally." Five short sentences compete for attention, and the same line is repeated in the browser tab and in search results.

In this preview

One promise in your own words, followed by your subheadline, so a remodeler knows in three seconds what you do and for whom.

2

Fix 2: Your strongest proof, sourced and up front

On your site today

"Roundtables members increase their annual revenue by an average of 40% in their first year" is a strong claim with no source next to it, and named member quotes sit further down the page.

In this preview

The 40% figure next to the headline with a source line, and a named member quote beside it. A sourced number is what both buyers and AI search tools trust.

3

Fix 3: Housekeeping that signals a maintained site

On your site today

Partner logos are labeled with file names like "builderfunnel-300x133-1.png", so search engines and screen readers can't tell who your partners are. The footer reads "Copyright © 2010 -" with no end year.

In this preview

Partners named in real text, and a footer year that updates itself.
