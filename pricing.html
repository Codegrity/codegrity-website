Pricing questions

Why do you publish your prices?
So you can tell in a minute whether we fit your budget, before anyone spends time on a call.
When do you quote a fixed price instead of hourly?
When the scope is clear enough to estimate honestly. Open-ended work, such as tracking down a production bug, is billed hourly with a range agreed up front.
What decides whether the rate is $100 or $125 per hour?
Scope and commitment. Ongoing monthly work sits at the lower end; short, urgent or after-hours work sits at the higher end. Your written estimate states the rate before any work starts.
