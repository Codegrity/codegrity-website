DailyPrimeStore

AI automation, our product

A fully automated AI content and affiliate pipeline. Every day it researches products, checks prices against their history, renders images and video, posts to the website and social platforms, and once a week sends an email roundup. Built on Astro, Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Actions and AI agents.

Gritfolio

Mobile and web platform, our product

A private portfolio platform for youth athletes and performers. Parents manage their children's portfolios and coaches and trainers can be discovered, while children never create accounts. We built the React Native mobile app, a TypeScript API on PostgreSQL, subscription billing through Stripe and Apple in-app purchases, and the marketing site.
