Senior engineering, without the agency layers.

Codegrity LLC is led by PJ Sojdei, who does the work himself, with two trusted developers he brings in when a project needs more hands. It started in 2023 in McKinney, Texas, to do careful, senior-level work for companies that can't justify a large agency, and it stays small on purpose.

PJ Sojdei

Founder

PJ is a senior full-stack .NET engineer with more than 15 years of building and supporting production web applications, APIs and SQL Server systems, from early-stage startups to Nokia. He has led a team of more than ten developers, worked as a solution architect on large-scale systems, and spent the last several years as the senior engineer companies call when an integration is failing in production.

He founded Codegrity in 2023 to work directly with the companies that need that experience most: ones with a small or overloaded dev team, an older system that still runs everything, or a backlog nobody has time for. He also builds Codegrity's own products, Gritfolio and DailyPrimeStore.

How we work

You talk to the engineer doing the work

No account managers or hand-offs. The person on your call is the person writing the code.

Written estimates before work starts

Hourly with a range or a fixed price, approved by you. Our rates are published on the pricing page.

Your code, your accounts

The code we write and the domains, repositories and hosting accounts belong to your company.

A reply within one business day

Email or call. If we're not the right fit for something, we will say so.

Notes you can keep

Every engagement ends with documentation, so your team or the next developer can pick it up.

Where we work

We are based in McKinney, Texas. We meet clients in person around Dallas-Fort Worth, including Allen, Frisco, Plano and Dallas, and we work remotely with companies anywhere in the US.
