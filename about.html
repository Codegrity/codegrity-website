A small team of senior engineers in McKinney, Texas.

Codegrity LLC is three developers. We started it in 2023 to do careful, senior-level work for companies that can't justify a large agency, and we've kept it small on purpose.

PJ Sojdei

Founder

PJ has spent his career on the software that runs a business day to day. He was one of the first developers hired at Nerium (now Neora) in 2014, and by 2017 he was leading a team of more than ten developers there.

In 2023 he founded Codegrity to work directly with the companies that need that experience most: ones with a small or overloaded dev team, an older system that still runs everything, or a backlog nobody has time for. He also builds Codegrity's own products, Gritfolio and DailyPrimeStore.

How we work

You talk to the engineers

No account managers or hand-offs. The people on your call are the people writing the code.

Written estimates before work starts

Hourly with a range or a fixed price, approved by you. Our rates are published on the pricing page.

Your code, your accounts

The code we write and the domains, repositories and hosting accounts belong to your company.

A reply within one business day

Email or call. If we're not the right fit for something, we will say so.

Notes you can keep

Every engagement ends with documentation, so your team or the next developer can pick it up.

Where we work

We are based in McKinney, Texas. We meet clients in person around Dallas-Fort Worth, including Allen, Frisco, Plano and Dallas, and we work remotely with companies anywhere in the US.
