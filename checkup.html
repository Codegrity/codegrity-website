Free Business Checkup

Find what's quietly costing you customers.

We check your email, website and Google listing, then explain what we find in plain English. It takes about a minute. Fix it yourself with our steps, or let us handle it.

No sign-up. We don't save your results or contact you.

What we check

Problems most owners can't see from the inside, because everything looks fine from their own phone.

Email

Whether your email proves it's really from you (SPF, DKIM and DMARC), so it reaches inboxes and can't be faked. Send us a test email for the full check.

Website

The padlock, speed on phones, tap-to-call, what Google shows in search, how links look when shared, and when your domain renews.

Google listing

Whether customers can find you on Google Maps with the right phone number, hours, website, photos and reviews.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. You get the full report with steps to fix everything yourself. If you'd rather we do it, the fix packages have flat prices shown right in the report.

Do you keep my information or follow up?

No. Your results aren't saved anywhere, and we don't collect your email unless you choose to contact us. If you send a test email, our email provider receives it so we can read the security results, so don't put anything private in it.

How accurate is it?

Every result comes from public records or a live test, and we show the evidence under each one so you can confirm it yourself. Some things can't be seen from outside, like DKIM keys under unusual names; for those we say so instead of guessing.

Will a fix break my email or website?

The fixes in the packages are small, careful changes, like adding a missing record or a setting. We make them with you, check them right after, and explain what changed.
