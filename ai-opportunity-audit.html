Know what's worth automating before you spend on it.

The AI Opportunity Audit is a fixed-price review of how your business actually works. In 7 business days you get the automations worth building, ranked by time saved and cost, plus a plan you own whether or not you hire us.

Book the audit Compare the two versions

What you get

  • A written report covering the workflows we reviewed and the systems behind them.
  • Three to five opportunities, ranked by hours saved, cost and risk.
  • A fixed-price build estimate for each, and a no-build alternative where one exists.
  • Data and risk notes: which tools would touch which data, and what to watch.
  • A 90-day roadmap for the top opportunity.
  • A 45-minute walkthrough of the report with the engineer who wrote it.

Seven business days

  1. Day 1: Kickoff

    The discovery session. We learn the work, the people and the systems.

  2. Days 2 to 5: Review

    We look at the workflows and tools, test ideas and price the options.

  3. Day 6: Report

    You receive the written report to read before we meet.

  4. Day 7: Walkthrough

    We go through it together and answer every question.

A good fit if

  • Someone on your team spends hours a week copying, checking or reformatting data.
  • You have been pitched AI tools and can't tell which would pay off.
  • You want a plan with real numbers before committing a budget.

Probably not a fit if

  • You already know exactly what to build. Book a call and we will quote it directly.
  • You need a compliance or legal opinion on AI use. We are engineers, not lawyers.

Questions about the audit

How much does an AI readiness assessment cost?
Our AI Opportunity Audit is a fixed $1,500, or $3,500 for the Deep version that adds a codebase and integration review. The full fee is credited toward a build you sign within 60 days.
How long does the audit take?
Seven business days from the kickoff call to the walkthrough of your report.
What if the audit finds nothing worth automating?
Then the report says so, and explains why. Not building something is a valid answer, and it is cheaper to learn that from a $1,500 audit than from a failed project.
Do you need access to our systems?
Read-only access or a screen-share walkthrough is enough for the standard audit. We sign an NDA on request and only use access for the audit.
